Privacy notice

This notice describes the public PB&J Strategic Accounting website. Contact info@pbjsa.com with privacy questions or requests about information you have provided.

Contact and callback requests

The contact form collects your name, email address, phone number, message and optional package preference. Callback requests collect your name, phone number and preferred day and time. These requests are sent through Web3Forms so PB&J can respond. Submitting a request is separate from choosing optional analytics. Please do not send passwords, account numbers, tax documents or confidential financial records through these forms.

Chat assistant

When you send a chat message or select a suggested question, your message and recent conversation history are sent through a Cloudflare Worker to Anthropic to generate a reply. Chat text remains in the page during your conversation. Use chat for general service questions, and do not include confidential financial or personal records. Closing chat does not recall information already sent.

Optional analytics and your choice

Google Analytics loads only after you select Accept all or enable it in Cookie settings. It can process page visits, browser and device information, online identifiers and interaction data. The initial page address supplied by this site omits its query string and fragment. Advertising signals and ad personalization are disabled in the site configuration.

Reject optional keeps analytics off. You can reopen Cookie settings in the footer to change or withdraw your choice. Withdrawing after analytics has loaded reloads the page to stop that code. It does not delete information previously sent or automatically remove existing Google cookies.

A necessary cookie named pbj_privacy_v1 remembers your choice for up to 180 days in this browser. It contains the analytics choice and a version number, not a visitor identifier. If the browser blocks this cookie, the choice may only last for the current page. Google Analytics can set its own cookies after permission is given. You can manage stored cookies through your browser.

Hosting, fonts and reviews

Cloudflare delivers the website and handles technical requests such as IP address, requested address and browser information for hosting and security. Cloudflare performance measurement, where enabled in the hosting account, is separate from Google Analytics and advertising pixels. The Google Analytics choice does not control hosting services.

Most pages request fonts from Google Fonts, which receives the technical information needed to serve them. Review content is requested through this site's reviews endpoint, and reviewer pictures can load from Google's image services. These display services are separate from optional Google Analytics. Following an external review, map or social link takes you to that provider's website.

Requests and retention

Contact PB&J at the address above to ask about access, corrections, deletion or the handling of a request you submitted. Avoid including sensitive records in an initial email. This notice does not specify a fixed retention period or promise anonymity; handling of a request depends on the information and the systems involved.
